Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 July 2026

SignagePanel is a digital signage platform operated by Bite & Brand Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how you can control or delete it.

It applies to the SignagePanel dashboard (app.signagepanel.app), the SignagePanel Player app for Android TV and Amazon Fire Stick, and this website.

1. Who we are

Data controller: Bite & Brand Ltd
Contact: okan@biteandbrand.co.uk
Location: Southampton, United Kingdom

2. What we collect

DataWhy we collect it

What we do not collect

  • We do not use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not collect location data from your phone or TV device.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, microphone or camera data.
  • The Player app does not collect any personal data from people viewing your screens.

3. How we use your data

We use your data only to run the service you signed up for:

  • Authenticating you and keeping your account secure
  • Storing and delivering your content to your screens
  • Showing you the status of your screens
  • Processing your subscription and sending receipts
  • Providing customer support when you contact us

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to build advertising profiles.

4. Who processes your data

We use a small number of trusted providers to operate the service. Each acts as a processor on our behalf:

  • Supabase — account authentication and database
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage of the content you upload
  • Railway — application hosting
  • Vercel — website and dashboard hosting
  • Stripe — payment processing (see Stripe's privacy policy)
  • Google Analytics — anonymised website traffic statistics, subject to your cookie consent

We may also disclose data where we are legally required to do so.

5. Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.
  • Access to production data is restricted to authorised personnel.
  • Card details never touch our servers — Stripe handles payments directly.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Account data — for as long as your account is active.
  • Uploaded content — until you delete it, or until your account is deleted.
  • Billing records — retained for 6 years where required by UK tax law.
  • After account deletion, data is removed from our live systems within 30 days.

7. Account and data deletion

To delete your account and all associated data:

Email okan@biteandbrand.co.uk from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Alternatively, message us on WhatsApp at +44 7311 799407.

We will confirm your request and delete your account within 30 days. This removes your account, your locations and screens, and all content you have uploaded. Billing records are retained only where UK law requires it.

You can also delete individual content, playlists, locations and screens at any time directly from your dashboard, without deleting your whole account.

8. Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Access a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correct data that is inaccurate
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, email okan@biteandbrand.co.uk. We respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies to function, and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use the site. Analytics cookies are only set with your consent, which you can manage through the cookie banner at any time. The Player app on your TV does not use cookies for tracking.

10. Children

SignagePanel is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as UK adequacy regulations or standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page and notify account holders by email where appropriate.
